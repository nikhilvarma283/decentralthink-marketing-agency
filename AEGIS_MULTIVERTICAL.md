# AEGIS
## AI-Powered Patient Acquisition for Healthcare Practices
### Multi-Vertical Platform: Dental, Optometry, Chiropractic, Plastic Surgery, Dermatology, Cosmetic Services

---

## Executive Summary

Aegis is a multi-vertical patient acquisition platform that generates and executes personalized marketing strategies for healthcare practices using AI agents. The platform addresses a **$10B+ market opportunity** across **168,000 healthcare practices** in the United States.

---

## The Problem

Healthcare practices across all verticals spend **15-30 hours/month on marketing** with inconsistent results and minimal patient growth. 

**Current options:**
- **Expensive**: Agencies charge $2-5K/month + generic templates
- **Time-intensive**: DIY marketing requires 20+ hours/month with minimal expertise
- **No middle ground**: Gap between expensive agencies and overwhelming DIY

---

## The Solution

Aegis synthesizes a personalized **90-day marketing strategy** from a single 30-minute conversation, then deploys **AI agents** to execute social media, email, and referral campaigns. Practice owners maintain full control through simple weekly approvals.

**Result**: 20-30+ new patient inquiries per month

## Vertical Deep Dive

### 1. DENTAL PRACTICES
- **Target**: 2-4 dentist practices with 500-1,000 active patients
- **Pain**: Inconsistent patient acquisition, time-intensive marketing, low ROI on ads
- **Value**: 20+ new patients/month = $4K-10K revenue for $500 cost (8-20x ROI)
- **Agents**: Social content, patient emails, referral campaigns, review generation, seasonal promotions

### 2. OPTOMETRY & VISION CARE
- **Target**: 1-3 optometrist practices, 400-800 active patients
- **Pain**: Competition from big box (LensCrafters, Pearle), low patient retention
- **Value**: 15+ new patients/month = $2.5K-6K revenue for $400 cost (6-15x ROI)
- **Agents**: Eye health education, contact/glasses promotions, annual exam reminders, frame fashion content

### 3. CHIROPRACTIC PRACTICES
- **Target**: 1-2 chiropractor practices, 500-1,500 active patients
- **Pain**: Word-of-mouth dependent, retention critical, high acquisition costs
- **Value**: 15-25 new patients/month = $1.5K-7.5K revenue for $350 cost (4-21x ROI)
- **Agents**: Wellness education, injury prevention, insurance guidance, patient stories, referral bonuses

### 4. PLASTIC & COSMETIC SURGERY
- **Target**: 2-5 surgeon practices, $5M-50M annual revenue
- **Pain**: High-value patients, longer sales cycle, reputation-critical, aesthetic positioning
- **Value**: 3-5 high-value consultations/month = $15K-100K revenue for $1.5K cost (10-67x ROI)
- **Agents**: Before/after portfolios, aesthetic trends, VIP invitations, video testimonials, influencer collabs

### 5. DERMATOLOGY PRACTICES
- **Target**: 1-3 dermatologist practices, 2,000-5,000 active patients
- **Pain**: Medical vs. cosmetic balance, seasonal fluctuations, insurance complexity
- **Value**: 20+ new patients/month = $6K-20K revenue for $750 cost (8-27x ROI)
- **Agents**: Skin health education, seasonal skincare, acne solutions, cosmetic promotions, sun protection

### 6. COSMETIC SERVICES (Med Spas, Botox, Fillers, Aesthetics)
- **Target**: 1-4 provider med spa/cosmetic clinic, $500K-5M revenue
- **Pain**: Discretionary spending, trend-driven, requires lifestyle positioning
- **Value**: 10-20 new clients/month = $20K-200K revenue for $1K cost (20-200x ROI)
- **Agents**: Aesthetic lifestyle, results showcase, influencer partnerships, VIP loyalty, seasonal specials

## Core Features (All Verticals)

1. **Discovery Chat** (30 min)
   - Multi-session conversation understanding practice goals, target patients, constraints
   - Personalized context gathering

2. **Strategy Synthesis** (Automated)
   - AI-generated 90-day personalized marketing plan
   - 5 action items tailored to vertical and practice

3. **Content Agent** (2x/week)
   - Writes and schedules social media posts
   - Vertical-specific messaging

4. **Email Agent** (2x/week)
   - Patient nurture emails
   - Appointment reminders
   - Re-engagement campaigns

5. **Referral Agent** (Monthly)
   - Referral incentive programs
   - Referral tracking and analytics

6. **Review Agent** (Weekly)
   - Requests online reviews
   - Manages Google, Yelp, Healthgrades

7. **Seasonal Agent** (Quarterly)
   - Holiday campaigns
   - Seasonal promotions

8. **Approval Dashboard**
   - Practice owner reviews all actions
   - Simple approve/reject workflow
   - Full transparency and control
