# Aegis Product Specification
**AI-Powered Patient Acquisition for Dental Practices**

---

## Executive Summary

**Aegis** automates patient acquisition for dental practices by generating and executing a personalized marketing strategy using AI agents.

**The Problem**: Dental practices spend 20-30 hours/month on marketing with inconsistent results and minimal new patient growth.

**The Solution**: Aegis synthesizes a 90-day marketing plan from a single conversation, then deploys AI agents to execute (social media, email, referral campaigns) while practice owners maintain control through simple approvals.

**Result**: 20+ new patient inquiries per month (beta validation)

---

## Product Definition

### What Is Aegis?

A **software-as-a-service (SaaS) platform** that combines:
1. **AI Discovery Chat** (30 min) → Understand practice goals, target patients, constraints
2. **Strategy Synthesis** (automated) → Claude AI generates 90-day marketing plan
3. **Agent Execution** (ongoing) → 5 AI agents handle content, email, referrals, reviews, campaigns
4. **Human Approval Loop** (2-3 min/week) → Practice owner approves actions before posting

### Core Features

| Feature | What It Does | Benefit |
|---------|------------|---------|
| **Discovery Chat** | Multi-session conversation understanding practice | Personalized strategy (not generic) |
| **90-Day Plan** | AI-generated marketing strategy with 5 action items | Clear roadmap & goals |
| **Social Content Agent** | Writes & schedules Twitter/LinkedIn posts (2x/week) | Consistent online presence |
| **Email Agent** | Composes patient nurture emails (2x/week) | Retention & referrals |
| **Referral Agent** | Creates referral incentive campaigns | Turns patients into marketers |
| **Review Generation Agent** | Asks satisfied patients to leave Google reviews | Builds online reputation |
| **Seasonal Campaign Agent** | Holiday/seasonal promotions | Captures seasonal opportunities |
| **Approval Dashboard** | Practice owner reviews & approves actions | Full control, no autopilot |

### What Aegis Is NOT

- ❌ Automated ads platform (no paid advertising)
- ❌ Autonomous posting (requires human approval)
- ❌ Lead capture/CRM system (focuses on execution, not capture)
- ❌ Patient management software (not a practice management system)
- ❌ Coaching service (not replacing human marketing support)

## Market Entry Strategy (Detailed)

### Week 1-4: Discovery & Proof

**Goal**: Validate that dentists will pay for this

**Actions:**
1. Build prospect list (40 practices in 2-3 states)
2. Personalized outreach (LinkedIn + email)
3. Book 3-5 demos
4. Close 1 trial customer (free 2-week trial)

**Success Metric**: 1 customer actively using Aegis

### Week 5-8: Execution & Refinement

**Goal**: Prove the product works (generates leads)

**Actions:**
1. Onboard customer through discovery chat
2. Generate their 90-day plan
3. Execute first month (you posting manually)
4. Track: # of leads generated, customer satisfaction

**Success Metric**: Customer sees 5+ new inquiries, wants to renew after trial

### Month 2-3: Repeatability

**Goal**: Prove you can replicate success with customer #2

**Actions:**
1. Refine messaging based on customer #1 feedback
2. Close customer #2 (same playbook)
3. Both customers on full execution (approval-based)
4. Document repeatable playbook

**Success Metric**: 2 paying customers, $1000 MRR

### Month 4-6: Scale

**Goal**: Build repeatable machine

**Actions:**
1. Document exact sales process (messaging, objections, closing)
2. Hire sales contractor or part-time sales person
3. Target: 5-10 customers
4. Optimize conversion rate (track: emails sent → meetings → trials → closes)

**Success Metric**: 5-10 customers, $2.5-5K MRR, clear sales playbook
