# Aegis Customer Acquisition Plan
**Dental Marketing Automation Platform**

---

## Product: Aegis for Dental Practices

**Problem Solved**: Dental practices spend 20-30 hours/month on marketing with minimal new patient results.

**Solution**: AI-powered agents that automate patient acquisition, engagement, and retention.

**How It Works**:
1. **Discovery Chat** (30 min): Understand your practice, goals, target patients
2. **90-Day Plan** (automated): AI creates personalized strategy with 5 action items
3. **Agents Execute** (ongoing): AI writes content, schedules posts, runs email campaigns
4. **You Approve** (quick): Review & approve posts before they go live (no autopilot)

**ROI**: Beta practices got 20+ new patient inquiries in month 1

---

## Target Customer Profile

### Ideal Practice:
- **Size**: 2-4 dentists, 500-1000 active patients
- **Location**: US (any state, metro preferred)
- **Current Pain**: Low patient acquisition, inconsistent marketing effort
- **Marketing Spend**: Currently <$2,500/month (room to grow)
- **Openness**: Active on social media OR willing to learn
- **Decision Maker**: Practice owner or office manager

### Why They'll Buy:
- Marketing is their #2 pain after patient care
- New patients = revenue growth
- They don't have time to manage marketing
- They've tried ads/marketing before but inconsistently

### Who to AVOID:
- Solo dentist with minimal staff (too busy to onboard)
- Large DSOs (already have marketing department)
- Practices in rural areas (limited patient pool)
- Non-English speakers (not ready for Phase 1)

## Lead Generation Strategy

### Week 1: Research & List Building

**Tools**: LinkedIn (free), Google Maps, ZoomInfo (if budget)

**Steps**:
1. Search LinkedIn: "Dentist" + location (start with 3-4 states: CA, NY, TX, FL)
2. Filter by: Title "Owner" OR "Practice Owner" OR "Office Manager"
3. Extract: Name, email (if visible), LinkedIn URL, practice name, location
4. Create spreadsheet with columns:
   ```
   Practice Name | Owner Name | Email | LinkedIn | Location | Patient Est. | Notes | Status
   ```

**Sourcing Channels**:
- **LinkedIn**: Filter by "Dentists" in target states, 500+ patient estimate
- **Google Maps**: Search "Dentist near [city]", click practice, get email from website
- **ZoomInfo** (paid, optional): Target dental practice owners
- **Local directories**: Check state dental boards, local business registries

**Goal**: Build list of 30-40 warm prospects (with email addresses)

**Estimated Time**: 4-6 hours

### Week 2-3: Warm Outreach

**Channels** (in order of effectiveness):
1. **LinkedIn Connection Message** (highest response rate ~15%)
2. **Email** (medium response rate ~8%)
3. **Cold Call** (lowest response but converts best)

**Your Daily Routine** (30-45 min):
- Send 5-10 LinkedIn messages (personalized)
- Send 5-10 emails (use template below, personalize)
- Make 2-3 cold calls to practices who don't respond
